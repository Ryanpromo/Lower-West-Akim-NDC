About Lower West Akim

Rooted in community.
Focused on progress.

Lower West Akim is a constituency in Ghana’s Eastern Region, with Asamankese as its principal town. Our communities, branches and members are the foundation of a movement committed to opportunity, fairness and development.

The Constituency NDC brings people together to listen, organise and serve. We honour our shared history while working toward a better future for every resident.
